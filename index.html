FAQ

What clips work best?+

Phone clips from 4 to 30 seconds with your bike or vehicle clearly in frame. Parked walkarounds and riding or driving clips all work. Steady footage, good light and one continuous shot give the cleanest results.

What photo do I upload?+

For Background Swap, pick one of our places or use any photo of where you want to be. No photo? Make one with the Image Generator. For Bike Swap, a clear photo of your bike, and a few more from different angles if you can (side, front, back and close-ups of decals). More photos give better results.

Do I have to write a prompt?+

No. Your clip and one photo are enough. Turn on “Extra details” if you want to add things like “golden hour” or “gold wheels.”

How do credits work?+

Each plan adds credits every month. Video costs 3 credits a second in HD or 6 in Full HD for clips up to 15 seconds (longer clips use 10 in HD or 24 in Full HD), so an 8-second HD video uses 24 credits. A picture from the Image Generator costs 1 credit. Members can buy extra credits any time, and failed renders are refunded automatically.

Can I get a refund?+

Credits used by a render that fails are refunded automatically. Otherwise all sales are final, because every render uses paid AI time. You can cancel your plan any time and keep it until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I post my videos commercially?+

Yes. The videos you make are yours to post, monetize and use in sponsored content.
